How it works

Mix. Spray. Gone.

  1. 1. MixShake the bottle well so the oils and soap blend.
  2. 2. SprayOn a dry, sunny day, soak the leaves until they're fully wet.
  3. 3. GoneWilting in hours, brown in 1–2 days. Repeat on tough weeds.

Where to use it

Made for hard spots

  • Driveway & sidewalk cracks
  • Patios & pavers
  • Gravel paths & lots
  • Fence lines
  • Mulched beds (Citrus & Clove only, spot-spray the weed)
  • Spots where nothing should ever grow (Original)

Not for lawns or garden beds you want to keep: it kills grass and flowers too.

CAUTION

Use it safely

  • Kills any plant it touches. Non-selective, so keep it off grass, flowers and gardens.
  • Never mix with bleach or any other chemicals.
  • Wear gloves and eye protection. It can irritate eyes and skin.
  • Keep kids and pets off treated areas until dry.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

First aid

Weed-X

Citrus & Clove

Plant-based, salt-free weed and grass killer. Makes 1 gallon (3.79 L).

Formula (per 1 gallon)

Warm water1 gal
Citric acid powder1 cup (~200 g)
Clove oil2 tbsp
Orange oil (d-limonene)2 tbsp
Liquid castile soap2 tbsp

Label percentages

Citric acid5.0%
Clove oil0.8%
Orange oil (d-limonene)0.8%
Other (water, castile soap)93.4%

Directions

  1. Shake well before use.
  2. Spray on a dry, sunny day above 65°F with little wind.
  3. Spray until the leaves are fully wet.
  4. Don't spray if rain is expected within 12 hours.
  5. Reapply after 5–7 days on tough or deep-rooted weeds.

What to expect

Wilting within hours in full sun, browning in 24–48 hours. It burns down the top growth; perennial weeds with deep roots may need a repeat spray.

Best for

Driveway and sidewalk cracks, patios, pavers, gravel, mulched beds (spot-spray only the weed) and fence lines.

Storage

Keep tightly closed in a cool, dark place. Use within 30 days of mixing.

Non-selective: it kills or damages any plant it touches, including grass and flowers. Read the safety section before use.

Weed-X

Original

The classic vinegar, salt and soap weed killer. Makes about 1 gallon.

Formula

White vinegar (5%, or 10–20% horticultural for faster results)1 gal
Salt1 cup
Dish soap1 tbsp

Directions

  1. Stir until the salt dissolves, then shake before each use.
  2. Spray the leaves on a sunny, dry day with little wind.
  3. Soak the weed; avoid any plant you want to keep.

What to expect

Weeds brown in 1–2 days.

Best for

Driveway cracks, gravel and spots where nothing should grow.

Salt stays in the soil and keeps plants from growing there for a long time. Not for lawns, gardens or flower beds. Non-selective: it kills any plant it touches.
